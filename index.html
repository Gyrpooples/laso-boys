Welcome to Archipelago!

I have made this site as a way to help us (and any other discord boys) easily setup and keep track of our archipelagos. I've had a lot of fun already with the limited experience I've had so I imagine this site will be helpful to us all in the future as we play more games.

To anyone seeing this site note the style comes from the official archipelago site. This project is the cumulative effort of many talented people and I do not wish to claim any of their hard work as my own. They have spent countless hours creating a huge repository of source code which has turned their crazy idea into a reality.

Make sure to visit the official site for additional help setting up games and to checkout the full list of games .
